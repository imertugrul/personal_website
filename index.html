// about

Hi, I'm Muhammet.

I started teaching Computer Science in 2009 at Robert College, Istanbul — one of Turkey's most selective schools. Over fourteen years, I built CS programmes, coached robotics teams, and learned how to make abstract ideas land with teenagers who thought they weren't "computer people." In 2023, I moved to the International School of Stuttgart, where I've taught IB DP CS, AP Computer Science, and MYP, and built a Robotics & AI Club from scratch.

Every area of the work outside the classroom connects back to something I have been thinking about inside it for seventeen years: how do students and institutions actually learn and change? Curriculum design, AI adoption, and robotics coaching are different applications of the same question.
